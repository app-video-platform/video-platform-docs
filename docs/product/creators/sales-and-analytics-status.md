# Sales and Analytics Status

## Overview

Creators can open a Sales page from the app navigation, but the current Sales and Analytics screen is placeholder content.

This page sets expectations so creators do not mistake placeholder sales numbers for real reporting.

## Who can use this

This page is for Creators.

Administrators can also access the Sales route.

## What you can do

Creators can:

- Open the Sales page.
- See placeholder sections for transactions, customers, analytics, sales trends, regions, channels, categories, payment methods, and time periods.

## How it works

The current Sales page renders static sample content. The listed transactions, customers, totals, conversion rates, growth values, and sales trends are not confirmed as live product or customer data.

Analytics and Messages navigation entries are visible in the app navigation, but there are no complete standalone Analytics or Messages pages in the current router.

## Current limitations

- Sales values are static placeholder content.
- The page does not load real transactions, customers, revenue, or analytics.
- Checkout and payment are not implemented, so real sales reporting cannot be treated as available.
- Analytics navigation is visible, but a full Analytics page is not implemented.
- Messages navigation is visible, but a full Messages page is not implemented.
