# Marketing and Reviews

## Overview

Creators can open the Marketing area to review marketing-related screens for their products.

The current Marketing area is mostly an early workspace. Reviews are the most developed section: creators can view received reviews and filter them. Email, messages, live sessions, and overview areas are visible but not complete workflows.

## Who can use this

This page is for Creators.

Administrators can also access the Marketing route.

## What you can do

Creators can:

- Open the Marketing area.
- Switch between Marketing tabs.
- View the Reviews tab.
- Search reviews.
- Filter reviews by visibility status.
- Filter reviews by rating.
- Sort reviews by newest, oldest, high rating, or low rating.
- See review customer details, product details, rating, visibility, comment, and dates when review data is available.
- See whether a review has a reply.

The visible Marketing tabs are:

- Overview.
- Email.
- Message.
- Reviews.
- Live Sessions.
- What.

## How it works

The Reviews tab loads reviews for the signed-in creator and applies filters after the user changes search, status, rating, or sort controls.

Review cards show the customer, reviewed product, star rating, visibility state, comment, created and updated dates, and reply state. Cards also show reply or edit-reply controls when a review has or does not have a reply.

Other Marketing tabs currently show headers or internal planning content rather than complete creator workflows.

## Current limitations

- Reply Now and Edit Reply are visible on review cards, but a complete reply editor is not currently wired.
- Review card menu controls are visible, but moderation actions are not presented as complete workflows.
- The product filter control exists but does not currently provide selectable product options.
- Email campaigns are not implemented beyond a visible tab header.
- Messages are not implemented beyond a visible tab header.
- Live Sessions are not implemented beyond a visible tab header.
- The What tab contains internal planning copy and should not be treated as user documentation for available features.
