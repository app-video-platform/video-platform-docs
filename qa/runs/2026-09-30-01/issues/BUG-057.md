# BUG-057 — Email verification guidance is almost invisible on white cards

- Severity: Medium — authentication guidance is difficult to read.
- Cases: AUTH-007, AUTH-009 and UX-015; the observed states are scoped below.
- Initial environment: local frontend and actual backend/PostgreSQL, 15 October 2026. Deployed Guest confirmations on29October are recorded below.

## Reproduction

1. Log out normally and open `/auth/verify-email` without a token.
2. The page mounts the correct invalid-link paragraph and sends no verification request.
3. Inspect the visible card: its status text is almost indistinguishable from the background.

Actual computed styles: card background `rgb(255, 255, 255)`; paragraph text `rgb(248, 249, 250)` over a transparent paragraph background; 16px, weight400. The calculated foreground/background contrast is approximately1.0541:1. The screenshot independently shows the almost blank white card. The earlier expired-token screenshot shows the same visual symptom, while this measurement specifically covers the missing-token Guest state.

Expected: users can read verification status and next-step guidance under the current app theme.

## Cause and scope

The verification stylesheet sets the card background to white without defining a matching foreground. The body inherits `--text-primary`, currently `#f8f9fa`. The missing-token logic itself works: it selects invalid-link state with no verification call, Guest profile probes return401 and no identity is enabled. This visual failure is separate from BUG-007, which records false success after a rejected verification request.

AUTH-007 changes from fully assessed Pass to Fail; its earlier semantic-state Pass remains in the historical ledger. No application fix or deployment was made.

Existing evidence batch key: `local-verification-missing-token-readability-2026-10-15`. It contains the DOM, actual computed colors, safe request trace, contrast calculation and current style sources. Screenshots remain in task storage rather than creating another repository evidence file.
