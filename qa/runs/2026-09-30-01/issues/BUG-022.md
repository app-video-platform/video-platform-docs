# BUG-022 — Notification button has no accessible name

- Severity: Low — the notification action is unnamed to assistive technology.
- Status: Confirmed in authenticated Creator public header.
- Case: UX-018.
- Observed: 1 October 2026; deployed revision unknown.

## Reproduction

1. Open the public Product page as an authenticated Creator.
2. Inspect the notification bell button in the accessibility tree.
3. Activate the bell; the empty notification popup opens.

## Expected and actual

The notification button needs a meaningful accessible name. Instead it has no text, aria-label, aria-labelledby or title and appears as an unnamed button. The popup itself displays `No notifications to show` correctly in a fresh session.

Other roles, populated notifications and screen-reader speech were not tested. No notification or Product state was changed.

## Evidence

[DOM snapshot and button attributes](../evidence/creator-notifications-empty.json).
