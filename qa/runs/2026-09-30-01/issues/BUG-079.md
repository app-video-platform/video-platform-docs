# BUG-079 — Thumbnail uploader overflows mobile and tablet workspaces

- Severity: Medium — narrow-screen media controls extend beyond the viewport.
- Status: Confirmed on the deployed app in a fresh Course editing session.
- Case: UX-014.
- Role: synthetic Creator owner.
- Observed: 28 October 2026.

## Expected

The thumbnail uploader fits the workspace at375px and768px without requiring page-level horizontal scrolling to reach Browse files.

## Actual

The thumbnail dashboard remains750px wide with its left edge at33px, ending at783px. The document expands to783px at both375px and768px viewport widths. At375px the Browse files control starts at434px and is entirely beyond the viewport. The screenshot confirms clipped media content and chooser presentation.

Gallery and promo dashboards fit their available width:309px on mobile and702px on tablet. At1440px the Media panel and all three Browse controls fit, with no document overflow. Other tested Course workspace panels fit their respective viewport widths. This distinguishes the thumbnail panel from a general workspace overflow.

## Reproduction

1. Sign in as a Creator and create a disposable Course through Products→Add product→Continue. A fresh creation session avoids the separate saved-editor reopen failure.
2. Set the browser viewport to375×812 and open Media using the workspace tabs.
3. Inspect the thumbnail dashboard and attempt to reach its Browse files control.
4. Compare at768×1024 and1440×900.
