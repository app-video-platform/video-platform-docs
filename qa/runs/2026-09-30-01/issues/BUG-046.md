# BUG-046 — Initial homepage keyboard traversal skips animated controls

- Severity: Medium
- Case: MKT-017 / deployed Guest forward keyboard traversal
- Status: Confirmed; no application change
- Environment: deployed homepage, independent Guest browser, default 1280×720 viewport. Deployment revision unknown.

## Reproduction

1. Open a fresh Guest session at the deployed homepage without scrolling.
2. Focus Home in the primary navigation and use Tab through About, Pricing, Getting Started, Contact Us, Sign In and Register.
3. Continue through the hero's Launch Your Universe and See how it works buttons.
4. Press Tab again.

Expected: forward traversal includes the homepage's Product buttons, five feature buttons and lower call-to-action buttons.

Actual: focus jumps directly from See how it works to the footer's CosmicApp link. The five feature buttons have zero opacity and hidden visibility at the transition. The initial accessible snapshot also omits the Product and lower call-to-action buttons.

The jump causes scrolling and subsequent animation reveals. Once those complete, all twelve main buttons are visible. Shift+Tab from the footer then reaches See pricing and the lower Launch Your Universe button. This is an initial forward-order failure, not a permanent focus trap.

## Controls and scope

After normal scrolling, all five feature buttons accept forward keyboard focus and select their matching active state. Mobile feature focus also works after revealing that section. The fresh-session failure was reproduced independently of those positive checks; a preceding reload/focus diagnostic showed the same jump.

Current implementation initializes animated controls or their containers with GSAP `autoAlpha: 0`, then reveals them on scroll. That is consistent with the observed skip; deployment source identity is unverified. Signed-in roles and reduced-motion preference remain untested for this assertion.
