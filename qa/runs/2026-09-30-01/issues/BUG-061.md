# BUG-061 — Footer FAQ link has no matching page anchor

- Severity: Low — the FAQ link reaches Contact but does not position its FAQ heading in view.
- Case: MKT-012.
- Scope: deployed Creator browser, pointer and keyboard activation on 20 October 2026.

## Reproduction

1. Open the public homepage while signed in.
2. Activate FAQ in the footer.
3. Observe `/contact#faq` and the current scroll position.
4. Activate the footer FAQ link with Enter.

Expected: the fragment identifies the Contact FAQ section and brings its heading into view. Actual: no element has `id="faq"`; the FAQ section has an empty ID. After pointer activation its top is approximately -368px in a 720px viewport, with document scroll around 1002px. Enter repeats the missing-target state and leaves the heading above the viewport.

## Evidence and implementation

The existing consolidated browser batch contains key `deployed-marketing-footer-primary-navigation-2026-10-20`, including the measured target absence, section geometry and pointer/keyboard observations. Browser-reported URL evidence is retained separately from the scroll/anchor failure.

`footer.component.tsx` links to `/contact#faq`. `contact.component.tsx` renders `<section className="faq-section">` without the matching ID. The deployed DOM independently confirms that mismatch. No claim is made about Guest scroll geometry or other fragment links.

## Cleanup

Read-only navigation only. No Contact form submitted, agreement accepted, account created or business data changed. The temporary browser tab was closed; the existing account remains Creator. No implementation repair was made.
