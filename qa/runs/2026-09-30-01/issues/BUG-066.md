# BUG-066 — Long Customer identity overflows the detail page

- Severity: Medium — long identity text extends outside the narrow detail viewport.
- Status: Confirmed in the deployed browser; not fixed.
- Case: UX-010.
- Role: Synthetic Creator.
- Observed: 22 October 2026.

## Reproduction

1. Open the retained disabled responsive reporting Customer `6147f94d-5cda-58c3-ade2-8660d5df0a5e` from Customers.
2. Use a 375px viewport and open Overview, Purchases, Access and Notes.
3. Repeat at 768px and 1440px.

At 375px every tab has a 1506px document width. The long heading reaches x1506.33. The Overview screenshot also shows its long email extending beyond the panel. At 768px Overview has an 801px document width; the other three tabs fit. At 1440px all four tabs fit. The precise cause of the additional tablet overflow is not attributed here.

Expected: long supported names/emails remain contained or wrap without expanding the document. The fixture name is 152 characters in total, with each individual name field within 100 characters; its reserved example.invalid email is 112 characters. This disabled fixture cannot sign in.

## Scope and evidence

The list itself fits at 375/768/1440px and preserves an accessible full-name profile link. All four detail tabs activate with Enter and have matching tab/panel labels. Purchases and Access each show two contained records. These positive results do not remove the header/Overview overflow.

Consolidated packet: `deployed-populated-customers-sales-responsive-2026-10-22`. Private screenshot: `deployed-customer-long-identity-overflow-oct22.png`. Real 200% browser zoom remains unverified; no zoom result is claimed. No account, purchase or entitlement was changed during the browser checks.
