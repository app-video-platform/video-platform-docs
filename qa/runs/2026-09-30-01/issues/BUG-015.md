# BUG-015 — Collapsed Creator navigation loses accessible link names

- Severity: Medium
- Status: Confirmed; not fixed in this test execution pass
- Related case: UX-002
- Role: CREATOR

## Reproduction

1. Open Products at desktop width.
2. Click Collapse sidebar.
3. Inspect the accessibility tree and Tab through the seven navigation links.

## Expected

Icon-only links retain meaningful programmatic names for Dashboard, Products, Customers, Sales, Analytics, Storefront and Settings.

## Actual

All seven links lose their text and have no aria-label, title or SVG title. The DOM accessibility snapshot exposes unnamed links with destination URLs. Keyboard traversal reaches each link; Products correctly retains `aria-current=page`.

This affects identification of destinations for assistive technology users; keyboard reachability alone does not supply their names.

## Evidence

- [Keyboard sequence and accessibility snapshot](../evidence/collapsed-navigation-keyboard-names.json)
- [Collapsed sidebar](../evidence/collapsed-navigation.jpg)

The sidebar was expanded again after the test. No Product or account state was modified.
