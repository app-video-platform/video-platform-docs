# BUG-088 — Long public Download title widens the hero beyond the tablet viewport

- Severity: Medium
- Status: Confirmed; not fixed in this execution pass
- Related case: UX-015
- Role: Synthetic Creator owner
- Environment: deployed frontend, 29 October 2026

## Reproduction

1. Open `/app/product/091b4bcd-8e17-47db-a098-768dcadb4bc0`, the existing Published synthetic Download with a long unbroken title.
2. Use a768px viewport and keep horizontal scroll at0.
3. Inspect the title and price panel, then reload.

## Expected

The valid saved title wraps within the public Product page, and its price and actions remain within the available width.

## Actual

At768px the document is920px wide. The Product article spans x48–720 and the hero x60–708, but the hero-copy grid and title span x60–920. The title is860px wide and is visibly clipped on the right. The Free price heading also extends to x903. The same bounds remain after a full reload with the file-section heading loaded.

The four-width run also measures916px documents at320/375px. The document fits1440px, although that alone does not establish that every desktop element is unobstructed. The ordinary Course and Consultation controls fit768px, making this a distinct long-title case.

## Scope and likely cause

This is separate from BUG-012: the marketplace navbar fits768px while the Product body still overflows. The captured title has `overflow-wrap: break-word`; its containing hero-copy grid has computed `min-width: auto`. Current shared landing styles switch the tablet hero to `grid-template-columns: 1fr`, while the title has a860px maximum width. These observations suggest an intrinsic grid-sizing issue, but the exact deployed source revision is not established.

The initial confirmation covers this Published Download as Creator owner. Additional roles are recorded below. Other types, layouts, themes and native zoom need their own checks. No Product data, configuration, files or entitlements were changed.
