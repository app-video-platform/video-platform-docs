# BUG-024 — Consultation prices lose cents when saved

- Severity: High — persisted amounts differ from the Creator's entered price.
- Status: Confirmed in the deployed development database; no implementation changes.
- Case: PROD-018, supplementary exact-amount persistence assertions.
- Role: OWNER-A, CREATOR.
- Observed: 2 October 2026. Deployed revisions unknown.
- Draft fixtures: `e81643a6-ed56-4b2d-bec1-a52c5870f4a1` and `deba7b76-1d20-423a-9f69-98cea00ef8bb`.

## Reproduction

1. Create a Draft Consultation and open Pricing.
2. Enter a positive decimal EUR amount. The input supports `step=0.01` and displays a corresponding one-time summary.
3. Wait for the unsaved indicator to clear.
4. Verify the synthetic Product's exact price in the read-only database and Product list.

## Expected

Supported EUR amounts persist with their cents. A saved17.25 remains17.25.

## Actual

- Entering17.50 in the Range Validation fixture results in database18 and Product list€18.
- A separate controlled fixture displays€17.25 in the form after saving, while its database price is17.
- In that controlled fixture, a positive€0.49 clears the local price blocker but persists as0. This establishes that the mismatch can change positive pricing to zero, rather than only alter its displayed precision.

The deployed `consultation_products.price` column is `bigint`, with scale0. Course, Download and Membership price columns are decimal with scale2. The schema difference is confirmed and explains the observed loss of cents; exact backend deployed revision is unknown.

This was reproduced entirely on Draft synthetic Products. No checkout, purchase or publication occurred. Financial transaction consequences have not been tested.

## Deployed persistence confirmation — 25 October 2026

Owner API updates on the synthetic Hidden Consultation `fc04f4c0-b7eb-4183-8eba-28f71b053725` still lose cents: requesting EUR17.25 is followed by a fresh GET showing string price `"17"`; requesting EUR0.49 is followed by `"free"`. Independent read-only SQL confirms `consultation_products.price` remains `bigint`.

The original Hidden status, EUR15 price and other Product business fields were restored after the checks. Separately, explicit zero and negative publication attempts return HTTP422 with `Consultations require a positive price`; rejected updates do not change the fixture. This is the existing persistence issue, not a new issue or a claim that free Consultation publication works.
