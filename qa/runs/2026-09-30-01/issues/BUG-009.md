# BUG-009 — Settings placeholder validation does not appear

Severity: Low. Case PROF-009. OWNER-A, CREATOR. Deployed revisions unknown.

## Reproduction

1. Open Settings → Subscriptions.
2. Enter one character, then focus the tab to blur the input.
3. Enter 51 characters and blur again.
4. Repeat in Privacy and Notifications.

Expected: the placeholder input displays its local title validation errors (minimum 2, maximum 50). Actual: all three tabs retain both out-of-range inputs without errors. There is no submit action to trigger validation.

These tabs are cataloged placeholders, not functioning subscription/privacy/preference management. This defect concerns their declared local validation only. Privacy also renders a Notifications label; that label mismatch is observed but does not establish any privacy capability.

Evidence: [Subscriptions](../evidence/subscriptions-placeholder-boundary.json), [Privacy](../evidence/privacy-placeholder-boundary.json), [Notifications](../evidence/notifications-placeholder-boundary.json). Per-variation outcomes are in the results ledger.

Local source hypothesis: forms use onTouched mode but the input blur handler trims through onChange without calling the form field onBlur. The deployed source revision is unknown.

Cleanup: leaving tabs and reload clears synthetic placeholder values. No subscription, privacy setting, or notification preference was persisted.
