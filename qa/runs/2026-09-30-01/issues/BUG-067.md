# BUG-067 — Long identity and item text overflow the Sales ledger

- Severity: Medium — populated transaction text clips and expands the document.
- Status: Confirmed in the deployed browser; not fixed.
- Case: UX-011.
- Role: Synthetic Creator.
- Observed: 22 October 2026.

## Reproduction

1. Open Sales with retained synthetic two-item Order `608d9d5d-dab9-568c-89e2-3c98ac8e2453`.
2. At 375px open Filters, select Paid and Product “QA 2026-10-20 Revoked library control”, then close Filters.
3. Resize to 768px, 1440px and back to 375px.

The document measures 1417px at both 375px and 768px, and 1830px at 1440px. The mobile screenshot shows the long Customer and immutable item titles extending outside the transaction card, with identity text overlapping status. Expected: transaction cards and desktop ledger contain supported long text while retaining all order information.

## Positive controls and scope

Paid and Course selections survive every resize; mobile Filters retains count 2 and the list contains one matching Order. Enter on its order action opens the correct detail. Both item lines, €15 and €7.50, total €22.50, and both Access granted labels are present. The Order drawer itself fits at all three widths, with focus inside it. Escape closes it; filters were cleared and the default viewport restored afterward.
