# BUG-065 — Settings content extends beyond mobile viewports

- Severity: Medium — Settings tabs and form actions are clipped at narrow widths.
- Status: Confirmed in the deployed browser; not fixed.
- Case: UX-003, observed after selecting Settings from mobile management navigation.
- Roles: Synthetic Creator and Admin at 375px and 480px; short-name Creator control at 375px.
- Observed: 21 October 2026.

## Reproduction

1. Sign in as the approved synthetic Creator and open Products at 375px or 480px.
2. Open mobile navigation and select Settings. The navigation closes normally.
3. Inspect the Profile page without horizontally scrolling.

The document is 716px wide at both viewport widths. The Settings tab row reaches approximately x716. At 375px, the Save button ends near x411 and Social Media inputs extend to approximately x474. The screenshot shows clipped tabs/actions and a very narrow Public Profile introduction beside the action row.

The same measurements occur for Admin with the long synthetic name. After restoring the original short Creator name and reloading, the document still measures 716px at a 375px viewport. This is not attributed to the temporary long-name fixture. At 768px the document fits within the viewport.

## Scope and evidence

Mobile navigation opening, route dismissal and explicit closing work separately; this defect concerns the destination Settings layout. No profile form action or account deletion was submitted.
