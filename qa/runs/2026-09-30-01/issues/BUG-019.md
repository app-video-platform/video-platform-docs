# BUG-019 — Quiz Total points edits do not change the displayed total

- Severity: Medium.
- Status: Confirmed; no implementation changes.
- Case: QUIZ-016.
- Role: OWNER-A, CREATOR.
- Deployment version: unknown; observed 1 October 2026.
- Course: `cb045295-cf70-445c-99a7-46fc0618e58e`.
- Quiz lesson: `37dbef65-eb38-48ce-b4f9-2a7b913c8bca`.

## Reproduction

1. Open the Quiz lesson editor in the initial working Course creation session.
2. Add a second question with Auto-Split selected. Total points is 100 and question points are 50 each.
3. Replace Total points with 200, then with 50.

Expected: the editable total reflects the entered valid positive amount and updates automatic distribution, or explains a validation rejection.

Actual: it immediately remains 100 with no validation message. The underlying input is editable and has no min/max attributes. Manual Split separately allows question values 30/70 and calculates their total correctly; this does not repair the Auto-Split total field.
