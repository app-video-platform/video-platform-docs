# BUG-028 — Initially expanded Contact FAQ answer is hidden

- Severity: Medium — the default open answer is invisible until the user closes and reopens it.
- Case: MKT-015, deployed Creator/User Contact initial state and page revisit.
- Scope: real deployed browser presentation; deployment revision unknown.
- Fixture: OWNER-A, Creator and temporarily User. Static FAQ only; no support request, agreement, purchase or profile change.

## Reproduction

1. Navigate to /contact from the marketing navigation.
2. Scroll to Frequently Asked Questions.
3. Observe Where can I find my purchase history? with its downward chevron and aria-expanded=true, but no answer visible.
4. Close the panel, then reopen with Enter. The answer becomes visible.
5. Visit Help Center and return to Contact Us. The initial hidden answer recurs.

Expected: a panel marked expanded reveals its answer. Actual: its own region has inline max-height:0px and clientHeight0 on entry, despite aria-expanded=true. Reopening produces max-height:26px and clientHeight26. A second page visit reproduces the zero-height default.

## Evidence and limits

- [Screenshot](../evidence/BUG-028-contact-default-faq-2026-10-02.png).
- [Marketing batch](../evidence/signed-in-marketing-batch-2026-10-02.json): creator-contact-default-expanded-hidden, creator-contact-purchase-history-reopened and creator-contact-second-visit-default-hidden, user-contact-default-hidden and user-contact-second-visit-hidden.
- Earlier toggle passes concern pointer/keyboard activation, not initial answer visibility. They remain valid.
- One exploratory measurement used getElementById despite duplicated region IDs and selected the first panel. It is explicitly marked unreliable for the other panels. The confirmed result uses the purchase panel's own region and two live visits per tested role. No duplicate-ID defect is added in this batch.

## Verified local cause

LegacyExpansionPanel initializes open state from defaultExpanded, but initial maxHeight reads contentRef before the ref exists and falls back to0. No mount measurement updates that value. Contact marks its purchase-history FAQ defaultExpanded. This is a source-supported explanation; deployed behavior is independently observed, and deployed source revision is unknown.

## Cleanup

Only static presentation was exercised. OWNER-A remains Creator and was returned to the Dashboard. No implementation repair performed.
