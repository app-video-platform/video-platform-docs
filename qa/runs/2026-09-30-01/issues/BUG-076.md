# BUG-076 — Product picker selection labels squeeze titles into vertical columns

- Severity: Medium — eligible Product identity and descriptions become difficult to read.
- Status: Confirmed in the deployed app; not fixed.
- Cases: UX-014; observed during MEM-012 functional checks.
- Account: synthetic Creator; private Draft Membership only.

## Reproduction

1. Create a Draft Membership and open Content → Add content → Existing Product.
2. At a 1280 × 720 viewport, search for an eligible Product with an ordinary multiword title.
3. Repeat with an eligible Product whose valid title includes a long unbroken string.

## Expected

Product title, lifecycle state, description and selection control remain readable within the picker drawer. Selection labels wrap or otherwise fit without collapsing the identity column.

## Actual

The 496px drawer reserves an automatic grid column for the full visible `Select {Product title}` checkbox label. The ordinary title control has a 363.67px selection label and a title column only 1.33px wide; its heading is 633.45px tall. The long-title control has a 1495.78px selection label extending to x2381.78, beyond the 1280px viewport. Its title column is 0px wide and its heading is 3551.13px tall. The screenshot shows the title running almost one character per line and the selection label clipped horizontally.

Normal selection remains possible through the visible checkbox indicator. A label-center automation click cannot reach the long label because its center is outside the drawer; this tool observation alone is not the defect. The rendered clipping and collapsed title column establish the usability problem independently.

## Implementation and evidence

The current Product picker uses `3rem minmax(0, 1fr) auto` columns above its 768px breakpoint. The final column contains a shared checkbox with the complete visible Product title. The title column permits shrinking to zero while its heading wraps anywhere. This source behavior is consistent with the measured deployed layout; the deployed revision was not independently identified.
