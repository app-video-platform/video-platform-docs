# BUG-016 — Account-menu Logout is skipped by keyboard navigation

- Severity: Low
- Status: Confirmed; not fixed in this test execution pass
- Related case: UX-006
- Role: CREATOR

## Reproduction

1. Focus the account-menu trigger and press Space.
2. Tab through the available menu actions.

## Expected

Logout is a keyboard-reachable action with appropriate interactive semantics.

## Actual

Tab reaches ADMIN, USER, Dev Dashboard and Docusaurus, then leaves the menu for Collapse sidebar. Logout is a DIV with no role or keyboard tab stop; its parent is also a nonfocusable DIV.

The menu itself opens with Space, closes with Escape, and dismisses on outside click. Those behaviors pass; the defect concerns the Logout action.

## Evidence

- [Menu keyboard sequence and redacted snapshot](../evidence/account-menu-keyboard-actions.json)

No role was changed and no logout was performed. Other role-specific account menus remain separate variations.

## Deployed User confirmation — 22 October 2026

The approved synthetic User reproduces the skipped Logout action on its entitled Download page. Space opens the account menu. Five normal Tab presses focus ADMIN, CREATOR, Dev Dashboard, Docusaurus and then the page’s Download section toggle. Logout is a DIV without role or tabindex and is never focused. No role button or Logout action was activated. Enter activation, Escape dismissal and outside-click dismissal pass separately.

Consolidated packet: `deployed-user-dropdown-keyboard-2026-10-22`. Populated Cart and empty Wishlist keyboard controls were tested independently without collection or checkout mutations. Their Escape dismissal leaves BODY focused; the intended focus-restoration contract remains unresolved.
