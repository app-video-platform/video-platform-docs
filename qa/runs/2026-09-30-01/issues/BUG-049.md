# BUG-049 — Successful Creator checkout redirects to an unauthorized Library

- Severity: Medium — enrollment/payment succeeds but its success destination is unusable.
- Cases: CART-006, CART-007 and CART-012 Creator variations.
- Status: Confirmed in current full frontend and actual backend; no implementation change.
- Environment: current frontend `12765d4568796f8472124383a221b74d82850c5b`, backend `1b860413436d5f850af0ecc228c5b613ebae4836`, separate migrated local PostgreSQL. Synthetic non-owner Creator. Fake payments only; deployed parity unverified.

## Reproduction

1. Sign in as a Creator who does not own a Published Course.
2. Add the Course through Explore and open the normal Cart route.
3. Complete free enrollment, or complete the explicitly configured fake paid checkout.
4. Observe the success toast and destination, then reopen Cart.

Expected: successful checkout reaches an authorized destination where the Creator can use or inspect their newly granted access, or the app clearly prevents an unsupported buyer journey before completing it.

Actual: the backend succeeds and persists access. Cart clears and the app announces that Products were added to Library. It then redirects to `/unauthorized`, showing `401 - Unauthorized`. The same dead end occurs after correcting a rejected free Product and retrying a two-item cart.

The Library guard in `app-router.tsx` permits User and Admin only. Cart is available to authenticated Creators. Both successful checkout branches in `cart.component.tsx` navigate unconditionally to `/app/library/all-products`. Library denial by itself follows the current route policy; successful checkout directing the Creator there is the inconsistent flow.

## Verified effects and evidence

- Creator first-time free enrollment: HTTP201, one ACTIVE FREE_ENROLLMENT, no monetary order, empty cart and unauthorized destination.
- Creator fake paid checkout: HTTP201/order200, PAID order975minor, ACTIVE PURCHASE grant, empty cart and unauthorized destination. No real charge.
- Creator partial enrollment/recovery: A remains the same grant; rejected Hidden B gets no grant. Restoring B then retrying adds exactly one B grant, clears cart and reaches the same denied destination.
- Synthetic Admin comparison: free, partial-recovery and fake paid flows reach Library successfully and grants remain visible after reload.
